# TeachDiffusion Dataset Card

## Overview
A curated dataset of math teaching video clips for training video diffusion models.

## Collection Method
- Source: CC-licensed YouTube math teaching channels
- Processing: Whisper transcription → scene segmentation → quality filtering
- Annotation: Topic labels, gesture types, pedagogical step types

## Statistics
- Target: 5,000-20,000 clips
- Duration: 5-30 seconds per clip
- Resolution: up to 720p
- Language: English
